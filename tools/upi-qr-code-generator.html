Quick answer

How do I make a UPI QR code?

To make a UPI QR code, enter your UPI ID (like name@okhdfcbank), the payee name, and an optional amount and note. The QR code works with every UPI app, including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM. Download it as PNG and print or share it. It is free and never expires.

Should I add a fixed amount?

Leave the amount empty for a shop counter QR so customers type what they owe. Add an amount for a specific invoice or fee so the customer cannot pay the wrong value.

Is a UPI QR code safe to share?

Yes. It only contains your UPI ID and name, which people need to pay you. Remember that you never need to scan a QR code or enter your PIN to receive money.

Questions people ask

Which apps can scan this UPI QR code?

Any UPI app, including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay and bank apps.

Where do I find my UPI ID?

Open your UPI app and look under your profile. It looks like yourname@okaxis or 9876543210@ybl.

Do I pay any fee for using this QR?

No. The QR code is free, and normal UPI payments to individuals and small merchants carry no charge.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. The QR code stores your information directly, so it works forever and does not depend on our website.

Can I make a UPI QR code for my shop?

Yes. Choose UPI, enter your UPI ID and name, and optionally a fixed amount. Customers can scan it with GPay, PhonePe or Paytm.
