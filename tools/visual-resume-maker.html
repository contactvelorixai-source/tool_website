When should I use a visual CV instead of a simple CV?

Use a visual CV when a person will read it first: emailing a hiring manager, freelance pitches, design, marketing, media and startup roles, or sharing on LinkedIn and WhatsApp. For job portals that scan resumes automatically, use the Simple CV, which tracking software reads more reliably.

What makes a good visual resume?

One strong accent colour, a clear photo, short bullet points with numbers, and skill bars only for skills you can back up. Keep it to one page so the design stays clean.

How do I put my resume website online?

Download the website file, then drag it onto Netlify Drop or upload it to GitHub Pages. Both are free and give you a link you can add to your email signature and LinkedIn.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between Simple CV and Visual CV?

Simple CV is a clean, text-only layout that applicant tracking systems read best, ideal for large companies and government jobs. Visual CV adds a photo, colours, skill bars and a timeline, which suits design, marketing, media, startups and freelance work.

Is a visual resume ATS-friendly?

Less so, because designed layouts are harder for tracking software to read. Send the Visual CV when you apply directly or share it on LinkedIn and WhatsApp, and use the Simple CV for online job portals.

What is the website option?

It downloads a single HTML file of your resume as a mobile-friendly personal page. Upload it to free hosting like Netlify or GitHub Pages and share the link.

How much does it cost?

Can I switch back to a simple CV?

Yes. Use the Simple CV tab at the top. Your name and contact details are kept in your browser.
