    Resize, crop or convert: what is the difference?

    Resizing changes the number of pixels. Presets also crop from the center so the photo fits an exact shape, like a square Instagram post. Converting changes the file type, for example HEIC to JPG, without changing how the picture looks.

    What does 'Lock ratio' do?

    It keeps the width and height in the same proportion as the original, so faces and objects do not look stretched. Type only the width and the height is filled in for you.

    Which format should I pick?

    JPG works everywhere and is best for photos. PNG supports transparency and is best for logos and screenshots. WebP gives the smallest files for websites.

    Questions people ask

    How do I convert HEIC to JPG?

    Add your HEIC photos, choose JPG as the output and tap Resize & convert.

    What size is a passport photo?

    For Indian passport and visa forms it is usually 35×45 mm, which the preset sets to 413×531 pixels at 300 DPI.

    Can I keep the aspect ratio?

    Yes. Keep 'Lock ratio' on and type only the width or height.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Everything runs in your browser.

    How do I resize a photo for an Amazon or Flipkart listing?

    Choose the Amazon / Flipkart preset (2000 × 2000 px). Marketplaces prefer square images at least 1000 px on each side.
