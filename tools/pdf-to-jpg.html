Which resolution should I choose?

Standard (about 150 DPI) is right for WhatsApp, email and web uploads. High (about 220 DPI) keeps small text readable when zoomed in. Max (about 300 DPI) is for printing, but the files are larger.

JPG or PNG?

JPG gives smaller files and is best for scanned pages and photos. PNG is lossless and keeps sharp edges on text, charts and diagrams, but files are bigger.

Why convert a PDF to an image at all?

Many portals and apps accept only images, such as a certificate upload field or an Instagram post. Converting also lets you share one page of a long PDF without sending the whole document.
