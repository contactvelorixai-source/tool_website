    What is OCR?

    OCR (optical character recognition) is software that recognises letters in a picture and turns them into real text. It lets you edit, search and copy text from photos of notes, books, receipts and screenshots.

    How do I get the most accurate result?

    Take the photo straight on, in good light, with the text filling most of the frame. Crop away anything that is not text. Clear printed text gives the best results; handwriting is harder.
