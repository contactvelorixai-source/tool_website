Quick answer

How do I count words?

To count words, paste or type your text in the box. The word counter instantly shows words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time, plus your most repeated words. It works with English, Hindi and other Indian languages, and nothing is uploaded.

How many words is a 2-minute speech?

At a natural speaking pace of about 140 words per minute, a 2-minute speech is roughly 280 words and a 5-minute speech about 700 words.

What are common word and character limits?

An X (Twitter) post allows 280 characters, a Google meta description shows about 155 to 160 characters, an Instagram caption allows 2,200 characters, and school and college essays are often 250, 500 or 1,000 words.

Questions people ask

Does it count characters with spaces?

Yes. It shows characters both with and without spaces.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes about 225 words per minute, and speaking time about 140 words per minute.

Does it work with Hindi and other Indian languages?

Yes. Words are counted by spaces, so Hindi, Marathi, Tamil and other languages count correctly.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser and the text is never sent anywhere.

Does it count numbers as words?

Yes. Any group of characters separated by spaces, such as 2026 or COVID-19, counts as one word.
