    How do I compress an image to 50KB?

    To compress an image to 50KB, add your photo and tap Compress. The 50KB target is already set. Fileora keeps the highest JPG quality that fits under 50KB, which is usually enough for a clear passport-style photo on exam and job application forms. Your photo stays on your device.

    Is 50KB enough for a clear photo?

    Yes. A passport-style photo of around 400 to 600 pixels wide looks sharp at 50KB. If your original is a large phone photo, the tool reduces its dimensions slightly to keep the face clear.

    What if the form also has a minimum size?

    Some forms ask for a photo between, say, 20KB and 50KB. A 50KB target lands just under the maximum, so it usually meets both limits.

    Questions people ask

    How do I reduce photo size to 50KB on a phone?

    Open this page in Chrome or Safari, choose the photo from your gallery and tap Compress. The 50KB JPG downloads straight to your phone.

    Can I compress several photos to 50KB at once?

    Yes. Add them all and you get a ZIP with every photo under 50KB.

    Is it free?

    Yes, free with no limit and no sign-up.

    How do I compress a photo to exactly 100KB?

    Pick the 100KB target. The tool finds the best quality that fits under 100KB and only shrinks the dimensions if it has to.

    Will compressing reduce quality?

    Slightly, but at 70–85% quality the difference is hard to see while the file gets much smaller.
