What must a GST tax invoice include?

Under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, a tax invoice needs the supplier's name, address and GSTIN, a unique invoice number and date, the buyer's name and address (and GSTIN if registered), place of supply, HSN or SAC code, description, quantity, taxable value, tax rate and amount for each tax, and a signature. This generator includes all of these fields.

CGST + SGST or IGST: which one applies?

If the seller's state and the place of supply are the same, it is an intra-state sale and the GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. If they are different, it is an inter-state sale and the full GST is charged as IGST. Change the states in the form and the invoice updates instantly.

What is a GSTIN and how is it structured?

A GSTIN is a 15-character number. The first 2 digits are the state code, the next 10 characters are the business PAN, then an entity number, the letter Z, and a check character. The tool warns you if a GSTIN does not match this pattern.

Questions people ask

When is IGST charged instead of CGST + SGST?

IGST applies when the seller and buyer are in different states (inter-state supply). Within the same state, the tax is split equally into CGST and SGST.

Is this invoice valid for GST?

It includes the fields a tax invoice needs under GST rules: GSTIN, invoice number and date, place of supply, HSN/SAC, taxable value and tax breakup. Check with your CA for special cases like e-invoicing.

How many digits of HSN code should I use?

Businesses with annual turnover up to ₹5 crore generally show 4-digit HSN codes on B2B invoices, and above ₹5 crore 6 digits. Check with your CA for your exact case.

How do I calculate GST from a price that already includes GST?

Use the GST calculator below the invoice and choose 'Including GST'. For 18% GST, the net price is the total divided by 1.18.
