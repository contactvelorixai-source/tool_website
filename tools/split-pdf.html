Split PDF

Keep only the pages you need, like 1-3 and 7, or split every page into its own PDF.

Files are processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    Mode
    Each range becomes its own PDF. Use commas to separate.
    Quick answer

    How do I split a PDF?

    To split a PDF, add the file and type the pages you want, such as 1-3, 5. Each range becomes its own PDF, or tick one-file mode to keep the chosen pages together. You can also split every page into a separate file. Fileora is free and works without uploading.

    Split PDF in 3 steps

    Add a PDF

    Drop the PDF you want to split.

    Choose pages

    Type ranges like 1-3, 7 or split every page.

    Download

    Get one PDF or a ZIP of all parts.

    How do I write page ranges?

    Use a dash for a range and commas between parts. For example 1-3, 7, 10-12 creates three PDFs: pages 1 to 3, page 7, and pages 10 to 12.

    How do I extract one page from a PDF?

    Type the page number, for example 4, and tap Split PDF. You get a PDF with only that page.
